# Dark Cosmic Archive v1.0 Freeze Report

## Status

**APPROVED**

The `dark_cosmic_archive` visual deck is frozen at v1.0 for its complete Major
Arcana set. The deck metadata status is `approved_full_major_arcana`.

## Major Arcana

**22/22** approved PNG assets are present in `major/`, with continuous
`00`–`21` naming. All audit targets are `1024×1536` pixels in a 2:3 vertical
ratio and are registered in `deck_metadata.json` without duplicate card IDs or
numbers.

## Visual Identity

- Cosmic archive: preserved astronomical records, star charts, and archival order.
- Ancient tarot: symbolic, painterly, readable card faces with ritual gravity.
- Celestial atmosphere: deep space, muted nebulae, antique-gold light, and quiet mystery.

## Frozen Rules

- **Color palette:** deep space navy; low-saturation nebula violet; aged
  antique gold; ivory white.
- **Border:** restrained antique-gold double line with sparse star-map and
  orbit decoration.
- **Typography:** Roman numeral at top; uppercase English and Chinese titles
  at bottom; fixed tracking and title whitespace.
- **Lighting:** one coherent ancient celestial focal light with painterly,
  archival contrast.
- **Card ratio:** `1024×1536`, 2:3 vertical.

## Future Extension

Future **Minor Arcana** and any **Alternative Deck Skins** must inherit this
frozen v1.0 contract, or declare a distinct visual deck ID and explicit new
version before changing its palette, border, typography, lighting, or asset
generation rules.
